What is Dollar?

Dollar (DOLLAR) is a digital token issued by Technologynine on the Ethereum network. It is designed to be backed by gold held in reserve, in the same spirit as the United States dollar before gold convertibility ended in 1971: new Dollar is minted only against gold in reserve, and the total supply never exceeds the gold backing it.

Design specification: 1 DOLLAR is matched to 8,009 micrograms of gold in reserve.

Current status. The Dollar smart contract is deployed and source-verified on Ethereum mainnet. Gold reserve arrangements, independent audit, and seeding are in preparation. Dollar is not yet offered for public sale, and no public offering has been made.

How the backing works

  • Gold first. Dollar is minted only against gold held in reserve in vaults. No gold, no new Dollar.
  • Supply cap by reserve. Total supply never exceeds the attested gold reserve, at the design rate of 8,009 micrograms of gold per Dollar.
  • Proof, published. Reserve attestations are to be published so anyone can check that supply stays within the gold held.
  • Redemption by design. Redemption works by burning Dollar and paying out against the reserve in a published window.

Whitepaper (summary)

Abstract. Dollar is a gold-backed token: a digital unit whose supply is tied, one for one, to gold held in reserve. It is built for people who want a digital dollar whose issuance rule is simple and checkable — supply follows the reserve, never the other way round.

The supply rule. The entire monetary policy of Dollar is one rule: total supply equals attested gold in reserve, converted at 8,009 micrograms per Dollar. Issuance beyond the reserve is not permitted by policy, and the reserve is held with custodians in vaults.

Reserves and attestation. Gold reserves are to be held in vaults and attested by independent parties. Attestation reports will be published on this page as they are produced, so holders can verify the backing rather than trust a claim.

Security. The Dollar contract is deployed on Ethereum mainnet with its source code publicly verified. An independent security audit of the contract system is in progress. Administrative actions on the live system are owner-signed only.

Roadmap. (1) Contract deployed and verified — done. (2) Independent audit. (3) Gold reserve and custodian arrangements. (4) Seeding and first public availability, only after steps 2 and 3 are complete.
